1.0 Launch

1.0 is out now

On October 19, 2026, Sortini: Ball Sort Puzzle officially launched on Google Play. It's the ball sort puzzle you know — gather each color into one tube — except in Sortini, the tubes come in different sizes. Use the small tube as a temporary holder, or empty the big one first? Every level asks for a new plan.

Features

What's in 1.0

  • Mixed-size tubes2, 4, 6 and 8-ball tubes in the same puzzle.
  • 1,000 levelsFrom relaxing warm-ups to real brain-teasers. Every level can be solved without items.
  • HARD & EXTREMEA HARD level at the end of each chapter, and rare EXTREME challenges.
  • Crown challengeSolve faster to earn a better crown.
  • LeaderboardCompete with other players on the Google Play Games leaderboard.
  • 3 helper itemsUndo (take back a move) · Extra Tube (one more empty tube) · Shuffle (mix the balls again).
  • Retro pixel styleNight-sky pixel art, with ball colors tuned to be easy to tell apart even in low light.
  • One-handed portrait playEnglish and Korean, and a Remove Ads option.
