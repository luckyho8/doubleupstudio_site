MAZEHOLD — Account & Data Deletion

Developer: DoubleUp Studio

Delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open MAZEHOLD and tap "Option" in the lobby.
  2. Tap "Delete Account", then "DELETE" in the confirmation. Sign in first if asked.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you can't open the game

Email contact@doubleupstudio.com with the subject "MAZEHOLD account deletion" and your Google Play Games display name (the name shown in the game's rankings). We delete your data within 30 days after verifying the request and reply to you.

Data that is deleted

  • Your game server account (created when you sign in with Google Play Games)
  • Your cloud save (backup of game progress used when changing phones)
  • Your stage and raid ranking records (display name and scores)

Data that is not deleted

  • Your Google account itself.
  • Game progress stored on your device — clear it with "Reset data" in the game or by uninstalling.
  • Purchase records — managed by Google Play and kept as required by law.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and crash logs (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) — not linked to your account and deleted automatically after the retention period in our privacy policy.
  • Google Play Games leaderboard entries — managed by Google; you can hide them in the Play Games app profile settings.

To remove only your ranking records, use "Option → Delete Ranking Record" in the game (your account and cloud save stay).
