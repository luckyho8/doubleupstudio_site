About

Don't block it. Bend it.

In MAZEHOLD you don't wall the monsters off. You place towers to build the road they have to walk — and where you put a single tower changes everything.

There's only one gate. Hold it for 100 waves.

Features

What's inside

  • Towers are the mazeBend the path, send the horde the long way around.
  • Switch the pathOpen one door, close the other, and keep them walking back and forth.
  • 32 towersFlamethrowers, chain lightning, hammers, magnets, webs, lasers, homing missiles…
  • 4 God towersChronos stops time, Hermes hastes your towers, Zeus calls down divine lightning, and the Divine Shot.
  • Tower gachaRare, Super Rare, Hero and God.
  • 16 gatesStorm, Mirror, Void, Time… each with its own power.
  • 2 stages × 100 wavesA boss every 10 waves, new gimmick monsters in Stage 2.
  • Cyber-retro neon pixelsEnglish and Korean.
