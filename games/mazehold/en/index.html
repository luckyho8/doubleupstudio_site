Game Guide

Game Guide

Build the maze. Hold the gate. Every tower you place is also a wall, so it reshapes the path. The longer the road, the longer the monsters are under fire. There is one gate — hold it for 100 waves.

  • A tower defense where you build the mazeCannons, lasers, flames, hammers, magnets, reapers… Monsters that split, hide, heal and teleport, plus bosses.
  • 100 waves per stageOuter Circuit, Core Sector and Cross Circuit. Waves you don't clear in time pile onto the next one.
  • A new raid every weekTake on this week's bosses and climb the leaderboard.
  • Gacha, Boost and DeckCollect and power up towers and gates to build your own defense. The Codex shows every tower and monster in motion.

How to play

How to play

  1. Build a maze with towersSpend gold to place towers during a battle. Each tower becomes a wall, and monsters find their own way from the entrance to your gate. You can't block the path completely, so bend it as long as you can.
  2. Waves and the timerEvery wave has a timer. When it runs out, the next wave spawns and piles onto the last one. Ready early? Call it in with "NEXT WAVE".
  3. Hold the gateMonsters that reach the gate lower its "Gate HP". On the final wave, defeat every monster on the map before time runs out to clear the stage.
  4. TicketsTapping "Start" uses one ticket. Get tickets daily and from ads, or buy them with rubies.
  5. Deck and GateIn the lobby, choose the towers you bring in "Deck" and the gate you defend in "Gate". Unlock more deck slots and gates with rubies.
  6. Gacha and StorageDraw new towers in "Gacha" (it unlocks after you clear wave 20). Duplicates go to "Storage" as cards.
  7. BoostIn "Boost", power up towers with rubies ("Ruby Boost") or with Storage cards ("+1 from duplicate"). You can also get rubies in the "Ruby Shop".

Modes

Modes

  • Raid

    A new set of bosses every week. Build a maze on a fixed gold budget and deal as much damage as you can to bosses that never die before time runs out. The run ends if a boss reaches the gate. Earn rubies based on your weekly ranking tier.

    The more damage you deal to bosses, the higher your score. Hitting all three scores more than piling on one.

    During a run, your current score, star count and weekly best show at the top left, and damage numbers pop above bosses as you hit them.

  • ∞ Endless mode

    Clear a stage to keep going from wave 101 with no end. Over time, towers break one by one and rocks fill their cells (clear them with gold). Further in, black holes swallow towers. Compete on how far you get.
