Guarantee and pity rules

  • 10 draws guarantee at least one Super rare or better. If the first 9 cards have none, the 10th card is decided with these odds: Super rare 66.6667% · Hero 26.6667% · Divine 6.6667%.
  • Pity: if 100 draws in a row bring no Divine, draw #100 is a guaranteed Divine, and you choose one Divine tower you don't have yet (if you have them all, you choose from every Divine tower). The count restarts whenever a Divine appears, by pity or not. The remaining count is shown at the top of the gacha screen as "Divine within N draws".
  • Including pity, it takes about 63.4 draws on average to get one Divine (effective odds about 1.58%).
  • Towers that do not come out of the gacha: Divine Shot (given as the Stage 1 first-clear reward)
  • The first free draw is always Ice Tower and is not counted toward pity.
  • All towers within a grade have the same odds. Individual odds are shown to 4 decimal places, so the rounded values may not add up to exactly 100%.
  • If odds or rules change, we will announce it in the game and on this page before the change and record it in the history below.
